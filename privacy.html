1. What Parchi is

Parchi is a billing and point-of-sale app for restaurants, cafes, shops and other small businesses. A business uses it to take orders, print kitchen tickets and bills, accept payments, manage items, stock, customers and staff, and see sales reports. It works offline on the device and can back up to our servers when the business owner turns online backup on.

"You" means the person using Parchi: a business owner, manager, cashier, waiter or other staff member. "We" means the developer named above.

2. Data we collect, and why

Account and staff data. Your name, email address and password. We never see or store your password in readable form; our sign-in provider handles it. Staff can also have a role, a branch and an approval PIN, which we store only in scrambled (hashed) form. A phone number is optional. Purpose: create your account, sign you in, and control who in the business can do what.

Business data. Business and branch names, address, phone number, GST number and UPI ID if you add them, tax and charge settings, menu and items, tables, stock, orders and bills, the payment method on each order, discounts, sales reports, and an audit log of staff actions. Purpose: run the features you ask for.

Your customers' data. If you save a customer (mobile number, and optionally name, email and address), that information belongs to your business and you decide what to enter. We store it only to provide Parchi to you. We do not use it for our own marketing and we do not contact your customers.

Device data. A random device ID that Parchi creates itself, a device name, and sync counters, so Parchi can tell your devices apart and show backup status. This is not your phone's hardware ID and not an advertising ID.

Crash reports. If Parchi crashes or hits a serious error, Google Firebase Crashlytics receives a crash report: the error and where it happened in the app, your phone model, Android version, app version, how much memory and storage was free, a Firebase installation ID, your staff ID (a random code, never your name, email or phone number) and the last few app messages before the crash. These messages never contain passwords, PINs or login tokens. Purpose: find and fix bugs. Crash reports are not used for advertising and are kept by Firebase for 90 days.

Usage analytics. Parchi uses Google Firebase Analytics to see how the app is used so we can fix and improve it: which screens are opened, which buttons and options are tapped, whether a form field was filled in and roughly how long the entry was (never what you typed), and counts and size ranges for actions such as orders placed. Analytics also receives your staff ID (a random code), the Firebase installation ID, your phone model, Android version, app version, language and approximate country. It never receives names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, passwords, PINs, item names, or exact amounts.

What we do not collect. Parchi has no ads, no advertising ID and no location tracking. The camera is used only on your device to scan QR codes; pictures are not saved or uploaded. Parchi does not read your contacts, messages, photos or files. If this changes, we will update this policy first.

3. Permissions Parchi asks for

  • Camera: scan a QR code to connect devices or scan a barcode. Processed on the device only.
  • Bluetooth (and, on Android 12 or older only, location): find and connect to your Bluetooth receipt printer. Android requires the location permission for Bluetooth scanning on those versions. Parchi does not read or store your location.
  • Network and local network: sync with your other devices over your shop's Wi-Fi and, if turned on, with our servers.
  • Notification and background service: keep the connection between your shop's devices running while the main phone is in use.

4. Where data is stored, and who handles it

  • On your device. Parchi keeps your data in a local database, so it works without internet.
  • Online backup (optional). If the owner turns on online backup, the business data above is copied to our cloud database so it is safe if a phone is lost and so more than one device can share it. It is off until the owner turns it on. Your account details (name, email, business and staff setup) are on our servers from the moment you sign up.
  • Hosting provider. Our servers run on Supabase, which stores and processes data for us. Supabase may not use the data for its own purposes.
  • Google Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics) receives the crash reports and usage data described above.
  • Google. Android may use Google Play Services to suggest your phone number at sign-up. Google Play delivers the app. Android's own device backup may copy the app's local data to your Google account if you have device backup turned on; you control that in your phone settings.
  • Your shop's network. When your devices sync with each other over your Wi-Fi, data travels between those devices on your network and does not pass through us. Parchi uses an encrypted link between devices where both support it. For compatibility with older versions, a plain link inside your own network can also exist.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers. We share data only with the providers above in order to run Parchi, or when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, it is erased 30 days after you ask. Files you exported or backed up yourself are yours and we do not touch them. We may keep a record that the law requires us to keep (for example, an invoice we issued to you for a paid plan), and we will say so when it applies.

6. Delete your account and your data

In the app: Settings > Business details > Delete account (owner and admin), or More > Delete account (everyone else). You enter your password to confirm.

Without the app: use our Delete account page or email getparchi@gmail.com from the email address of the account.

  • The account stops working at once. Your data is erased 30 days after you ask. Until then you can sign in and choose "Restore account".
  • Owner: the business and everything in it is erased: orders, bills, menu, customers, tables, tax settings, stock, staff members and the logins of staff who belong to no other business.
  • Staff member: your login and name are erased. The business keeps its records, and your name on them shows as "Deleted user".
  • We keep no copy of deleted data after the 30 days.

7. Your rights and complaints

You may ask us to show you your data, correct it, erase it, or withdraw your consent, and you may nominate another person to act for you if you cannot. Withdrawing consent can mean we can no longer provide the app to you. Write to getparchi@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Grievance contact: Parchi support, getparchi@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operating.

8. Security

Data between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Approval PINs are hashed. Access to business data is limited by account and role. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your password and PIN private and sign out of shared devices.

9. Children

Parchi is built for businesses and is not meant for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
