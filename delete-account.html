Delete your Parchi account

App: Parchi · Privacy Policy

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Parchi and sign in.
  2. Owner or admin: go to Settings > Business details > Delete account. Everyone else: go to More > Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and confirm.

Option 2: without the app

Email getparchi@gmail.com from the email address of your Parchi account, with the subject "Delete my Parchi account". Include your name and your business name. We may ask you one question to confirm it is you. We start the deletion within 7 days and reply when it is scheduled.

What happens

  • The account stops working at once.
  • Your data is erased 30 days after the request. Until then you can sign in and choose "Restore account".
  • If you are the owner: the business and its data are erased: orders, bills, menu, customers, tables, tax settings, stock and staff members, together with the logins of staff who belong to no other business.
  • If you are staff: your login and name are erased. The business keeps its records, and your name on them shows as "Deleted user".
  • We keep no copy of deleted data after the 30 days. Files you exported or backed up yourself are yours and stay where you saved them.

Only deleting the account removes your data. Signing out, uninstalling the app or turning online backup off does not erase what is on our servers.
